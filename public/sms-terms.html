SMS Terms and Privacy

Crest Air LLC · Heating & Cooling · Tucson, Arizona

How you opt in

When you submit a form on this website, request a quote or a service visit, or ask us to contact you, you are giving Crest Air permission to call and send text messages to the phone number you provided. The disclosure appears on every form, next to the submit button. You may also give permission verbally when you speak with us on the phone.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any product or service from us.

What we send

We send messages about the job you contacted us about: confirming your request, scheduling or confirming an appointment, letting you know a technician is on the way, following up after a service visit or estimate, and answering questions you ask us. Message frequency varies with your job. Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from us. You will get one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and we will not text you again unless you ask us to. Reply HELP for help, or call us at (520) 751-8888.

Your information

We collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, and what you tell us about the work you need. We use it to respond to your request and to perform the work.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share your phone number or messaging consent with anyone for their own marketing. We share information only with people who help us do the job, for example our scheduling and messaging providers, and only what they need.

To see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, email support@crestairaz.com or call (520) 751-8888.
